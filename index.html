About Me

I am a final-year B.Tech ECE student at KL University, specializing in 5G/6G networks, Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning, and robotics. My main projects include an Autonomous 5G Network Operations Center built on Free5GC and UERANSIM that pairs a Llama 3.1 8B model with a RAG pipeline for AI-driven network operations, an Open5GS-based 5G NOC with a web dashboard, and an autonomous rover built on ROS2 Jazzy. I also work on AI/ML approaches to base-station energy optimization and mmWave beam and blockage prediction for 5G/6G.

Alongside development, I contribute to technical education: I delivered a 5G and O-RAN lecture to faculty at IIITDM Kurnool as part of a Faculty Development Program, and ran a hands-on 5G workshop at NIT Warangal during an international conference. I am driven by research and innovation in AI-driven telecommunications and autonomous systems, and by building technology that solves real problems.
